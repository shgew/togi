# Two ends: clean daily holds earn bounded, night-qualified steps

Status: accepted; implementation pending. [Ruleset 13](../spec/tuner.md) remains the normative description of the shipped binary; the successor rules in [Ruleset 14](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md) are specified, not implemented. Consolidates and takes current-policy ownership from [0079](0079-day-observer-night-tester.md)–[0084](0084-a-fresh-ruleset-transition.md); see [the index](README.md).

## Context

Ruleset 13 was decided in [#1214](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1214) and [#1215](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1215) and recorded as six decisions: days observe and nights test ([0079](0079-day-observer-night-tester.md)), one offset per CCD first ([0080](0080-per-ccd-first.md)), a cap from measured slope ([0081](0081-margin-from-measured-slope.md)), BIOS Curve Optimizer at zero ([0082](0082-bios-stays-at-zero.md)), questions after unclean daily ends ([0083](0083-ask-after-unclean-ends.md)) and a fresh transition ([0084](0084-a-fresh-ruleset-transition.md)). It shipped in v0.12.0. **Its registered day/night gate ([#1216](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1216), [#1217](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1217)) failed: Ruleset 13 was shipped, not gate-accepted.** That registration and its failing results stay frozen as a failed experiment; nothing here rewrites them as a pass or reopens them for retrospective editing.

The failure is structural, not a shortage of exposure. Ruleset 13 makes the statistical cap S both the promotion destination and a retreat constraint. Clean daily hours are holds, not mapping-class rate exposure, so they cannot move S; when D = S = 0 the permission window is empty however long the machine runs cleanly. [#1217's stall analysis](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1217#issuecomment-6400502490) shows that, under the existing Poisson/log-linear model, ordinary edges commonly project to S = 0 at λ* = 1 crash per 3,000 machine-hours, and that directly supporting the per-unit share with zero failures would take thousands of class-hours. The registered gate also required proximity within one count of a censored −50 optimum while the U+5 margin permits at most −45, a contradiction more exposure cannot repair.

[#1458](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1458) decides the successor policy, called D, on 2026-10-29. The companion [successor gate #1457](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1457) owns its registration and scoring. This record states why D was chosen and which parts of the six Ruleset 13 decisions survive; [the Ruleset 14 specification](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md) owns every rule, threshold and witness, and this record does not restate them.

## Decision

### Bounded exploration replaces cap-as-permission

**Fifty clean daily hours on the exact applied profile earn permission to try one bounded step to a night-qualified profile beneath a hard night ceiling.** The ceiling comes from the isolated night edge plus a fixed margin ([§2](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#2-night-edge-and-hard-ceiling)); qualification is an exact whole-profile certificate under the unchanged coverage matrix ([§3](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#3-what-exactly-qualifies-the-next-profile)); the hold, step size and unit order are in [§4](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#4-a-clean-hold-earns-one-step). This is explicitly bounded exploration, not statistical certification. λ* stays the eventual goal for CO-added hazard and is not raised to make the climb pass.

This replaces [0081](0081-margin-from-measured-slope.md)'s decision that a statistical cap grants and bounds promotion. Low S, sparse exposure or a changed fitted slope can no longer block or undo a step; S may survive only as a labelled, non-authorizing diagnostic. Actual adverse evidence (a shallower observed edge, a failed current-profile qualification, an active veto, an invalid context) still retreats through its explicit path ([§7](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#7-reconcile-every-current-decision-path)).

0081's reasons against overconfident extrapolation still hold and now also limit what D may claim. A fixed steep slope, or a ratchet that accepts only steeper measurements, can overstate how fast failures disappear on a flatter chip; averaging across classes can hide a dangerous load; subtracting an uncertain stock hazard can manufacture apparent safety from noise. D therefore never presents a hold or a finished climb as a rate guarantee: displayed evidence is the exact-profile pointwise Poisson bound with its model stated, and “meets your goal” requires the current profile's own daily evidence to support λ* ([§6](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#6-finish-steady-use-and-the-words-shown)). 0081's reversal of [0038](0038-self-sufficient-cores.md)'s rejection of rate-based policy remains history; D does not revive 0038's failure-tolerance proposal, and failures remain raw observations.

### Days observe; nights test

**Retained from [0079](0079-day-observer-night-tester.md).** Daily use runs no stress workload and observes the applied profile; controlled nights map edges, reproduce events, run controls and qualify candidates; the pure tuner alone decides, and the journal, machine seams, containment and SMU transaction remain the execution boundary. Reserved night shares keep reproduction from consuming every night. What changes is the night's purpose: work maps and brackets the edge and qualifies the exact next profile instead of filling cells to unlock S. 0079's supersession of the per-core phase and checking policy of [0054](0054-two-phases-replace-the-deepening-loop.md) and [0056](0056-later-failures-step-back-in-pairs.md) stands; D revives neither phases, rounds, hunts nor checking cycles.

### Coarse first, then per-core continuation

**Retained from [0080](0080-per-ccd-first.md): one offset per CCD first, and request rank is not blame.** Hunts, failure points, combinations, voltage-target backoff and singleton/member probes stay removed, and joint failures stay profile-level until an isolated trial supplies evidence. Honest partial-load generation remains.

D adds the continuation 0080 left open. Rules apply to a tuning unit: a CCD in the coarse phase, a physical core in refinement ([§8](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#8-per-core-refinement-is-the-end-goal-not-a-restart-from-zero)). Refinement inherits the last clean coarse profile and its real ancestry, moves one core per 50-hour hold, and needs a core-specific isolated edge and fresh whole-profile qualification for every step. Coarse and refinement are two phases of one state machine, not a return to [0054](0054-two-phases-replace-the-deepening-loop.md)'s per-core phases or to requester blame. Per-core tuning is the end goal; its implementation is a separate merge unit under [#1222](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1222).

### BIOS stays at zero

**Retained from [0082](0082-bios-stays-at-zero.md), including its supersession of [0004](0004-find-only.md) and of [0006](0006-start-from-bios-values.md)'s BIOS-start choice.** Profiles apply at runtime at normal boot or resume while BIOS stays at CO 0, so a watchdog reset returns to the untuned baseline and every day step is a journaled application. There is no export to BIOS and no automatic BIOS recommendation; runtime/BIOS equivalence and early-boot safety are not shown. A firmware change resets to stock and invalidates active evidence. D adds one explicit exception for that case: after new-context stock controls, exact night qualification of the previous full profile and 50 clean stock hours, that exact profile may be restored as one learning step instead of re-climbed ([§7](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#7-reconcile-every-current-decision-path)). The exception grants no new depth and reopens no stopped unit.

### Newest-step stop

**[0083](0083-ask-after-unclean-ends.md) is amended.** Its revisable event classification and whole-profile vetoes are retained: an unclean end is evidence, not proof that Curve Optimizer caused it; orderly shutdowns are not crashes; answers stay revisable; independent machine-check evidence cannot be answered away; a CPU-suspect event vetoes its whole applied profile and every profile at least as deep on every core.

0083 rejected blaming the most recently changed unit because an older unit can survive a hold by chance. That reason stands, so D does not use newest-step blame as causal localization. D uses it as an expressly **non-causal stopping heuristic** ([§5](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#5-newest-step-blame-stop-and-the-machine-allowance)): the first CPU-suspect event reverts the newest applied step and permanently stops that unit; further events walk every unit back toward stock. A successful hold never exonerates an older unit, and the whole-profile veto still covers the profile actually running. The heuristic bounds how many daily experiments a doubtful step can cause; it does not claim to find the guilty unit.

The stop is paired with a durable machine-wide allowance of two unique CPU-suspect learning events, shared across coarse and refinement and never renewed by restarts, resets, firmware changes, phase changes or transitions. 0083 also rejected permanently charging every unexplained end, because background failures would consume depth without CO evidence. D keeps that protection for depth: a later non-CPU classification permits guarded restoration of the undone depth. It deliberately does not refund the allowance or reopen the stopped unit, because reclassification must not become a way to renew risk.

### Transitions

**[0084](0084-a-fresh-ruleset-transition.md) remains the history of the Ruleset 13 cutover**: that session discarded the per-core tuning session, imported raw daily observations and removed `togi-carry`. 0084 explicitly promised neither to restart future climbs nor to restore D across rulesets, and D does not read it as such a promise.

**D specifies its own transition** ([§10](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#10-journal-schema-and-architectural-record)): archive the Ruleset 13 session unchanged and start at verified stock; keep original-identity raw daily observations, applicable whole-profile vetoes and compatible night observations for explicitly checked recomputation; never carry S, a qualification conclusion or nonzero D as permission. Campaign allowance and unit stops are reconstructed across the transition so session freshness cannot renew risk. When required campaign history cannot be reconstructed, new nonzero exploration is blocked rather than assumed unused. The supersession of [0019](0019-a-ruleset-change-starts-a-seeded-session.md)'s seeded session and [0027](0027-carry-trial-facts.md)'s carried trial facts, recorded by 0084 for Ruleset 13, applies equally to this transition. Old-context facts remain history and hints.

### Gate and activation

The binary still implements Ruleset 13 until an implementing change lands with a separately authorized compatibility switch; this record assigns no ruleset, schema or evidence-epoch number. Automatic nonzero day offsets stay disabled until D's rules and allowance are specified, the frozen successor registration in [#1457](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1457) scores this exact policy with acceptance met or its failure explicitly disposed of, and the hardware acceptance in [§9](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#9-safety-and-activation) passes. Running a gate is not permission to enable nonzero use, and no score of D can retroactively accept Ruleset 13.

## Considered Options

- **Keep S as permission and add exposure, filler or pooled evidence.** Rejected: clean daily holds cannot open an S = 0 window, and filler near S−1 did not move S in reported runs. The pooled-evidence variant B′ also changed filler and onset treatment, failed the same reduced development comparison and is not a validated rare-event bound; it is not merged.
- **Raise λ* until the climb passes.** Rejected: that redefines the goal to fit the policy.
- **A fixed steep slope or a steeper-only ratchet to shrink the margin.** Rejected, as in 0081: either can overstate how fast failures disappear on a flatter chip.
- **Average across classes, or subtract an uncertain stock hazard.** Rejected, as in 0081: averaging can hide a dangerous load, and subtraction can create apparent safety from noise.
- **Causal newest-unit blame.** Rejected, as in 0083: an older unit can survive a hold by chance. Accepted only as the non-causal stop above.
- **Requester blame, hunts and probes.** Rejected, as in 0080; not revived for per-core refinement.
- **Retry a stopped unit from its last clean depth, or a per-unit or per-phase allowance.** Rejected: repeated daily probes after a failed step multiply learning crashes, and sixteen fresh allowances on entering refinement would hide the cost behind a phase label.
- **Restart refinement from zero, batch several core steps, or credit one hold to many cores.** Rejected: a sixteen-core restart from zero needs about 5,600 applied hours in the analytical example, and batching or shared credit hides which change the hold observed.
- **Re-climb step by step after a firmware change.** Rejected in favour of exact-profile restoration in one learning step after requalification and a stock hold; an ordinary re-climb repeats holds without new evidence about the new firmware.
- **Carry S, qualifications or nonzero D into the successor, or default a missing allowance to unused.** Rejected: old conclusions are not current permission, and missing history is not evidence of no events.
- **Keep Ruleset 13 as a selectable mode.** Rejected, as in 0079: two policies would hold competing definitions of evidence and promotion.
- **Export a profile to BIOS or start from BIOS values.** Rejected, as in 0082.

## Consequences

D deliberately permits exploratory profiles whose daily CO-added hazard may exceed λ*. A 50-hour survival is likely even at one failure per 100 h, and transfer from nights can fail through hidden workloads, interactions, shared voltage, firmware changes or wrong answers. The ceiling margin, exact qualification, newest-step stop and two-event allowance are safeguards, not rare-event guarantees, and evidence wording says so. Newest-step stopping can stop an innocent unit while an older one stays hazardous; whole-profile vetoes and escalation toward stock cover that case at the cost of depth.

Progress has a linear daily-hour floor. Under fixed ceilings, clean holds and ready certificates, the coarse example to C = −25 takes about 700 applied hours, and one to five further counts per core in refinement add about 800–2,400 hours ([§8](../spec/tuner-ruleset14.md#8-per-core-refinement-is-the-end-goal-not-a-restart-from-zero) owns the arithmetic and its assumptions). If refinement needs too many serial holds, that is a tractability failure to decide anew, not permission to shorten holds or relax coverage.

Until implementation, Ruleset 13 in [tuner.md](../spec/tuner.md) describes the shipped binary and this record describes the accepted successor. The disposition of Ruleset 13 is fixed in every place that states it: shipped in v0.12.0; registered gate not met; superseded by this design. Outstanding Ruleset 13 hardware acceptance moves to named tracking in [#1459](https://github.com/shgew/togi/issues/1459) rather than being waived.
