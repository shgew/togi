# Brief: #427, one structured single-core hazard

**Mode:** model development, under [the model program](../program.md) and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

Does a smooth, partially pooled single-core hazard transfer sparse core and workload evidence, and so predict later sessions' single-core trials better, without costing calibration, surprises or breadth? #427 decides the shape: a hazard smooth in offset, per-core intercepts shrunk toward a shared mean, workload effects shared across cores, and one limit for alone and together trials. The 2026-10-03 run (#306) tried variants: the shrunk smooth hazard failed F3, and the resident-aware form improved development but rose on the session then sealed, 20261002T004254Z, now a development session. Each candidate states which of those failures it addresses and how.

## Candidate queue

At most 12 candidates, written into the manifest before the first one runs and never extended from a confirmation result. The family varies only the pooling and regularization: shrinkage strength of the per-core intercepts (three predeclared levels), how workload effects enter the hazard (additive or multiplicative on the shared rate) and the offset shape (logistic or log-linear). Every candidate keeps one limit for alone and together trials, so it deletes the fitter's copy of the alone/together classification. No per-core free parameters beyond the shrunk intercepts, and no parameter keyed to a session, ruleset or profile.

## Bounds

Fixed before the first candidate runs; a candidate outside them is not run.

- **Queue:** three shrinkage levels by two workload forms by two offset shapes, 12 candidates. Shrinkage is a normal penalty on each core's intercept around the shared mean with a standard deviation of 2, 4 or 8 offset counts.
- **Parameters:** one intercept per core, inside the fitter's limit range of -50 to 1; at most two offset-shape parameters shared by all cores, each positive and finite; one effect per workload with at least ten trials (the fitter's existing minimum), shared by all cores. Nothing else varies by core, and nothing by session, ruleset or profile.
- **Machine files:** every existing key keeps its meaning. A new key is optional, and leaving it out is the current model.

## Fixtures

Every candidate passes these, apart from the two exceptions named under synthetic recovery, which a candidate that takes one removes as its record states; it may add tests but edits none of the others:

- **Synthetic recovery:** `crates/togi-tool-fit/src/tests/search.rs` fits trials drawn from known parameters and recovers them, including one limit shared by alone and together trials. A candidate adds a recovery test of its own parameters, intercepts and workload effects included, and passes it before its first forward run. Two exceptions, each named in the candidate's record when it takes it: the whole of `a_fit_recovers_the_limits_and_hazard_shape_of_the_machine_that_drew_the_trials`, whose distinct alone and together limits the single limit replaces; and, for a logistic candidate, the `past_limit_rate` and `growth` bounds of `a_fit_recovers_one_limit_shared_by_alone_and_together_trials`, which belong to the log-linear shape and which the candidate's own recovery test of its shape parameters replaces.
- **Absent keys:** `crates/togi-sim/tests/suite/compatibility.rs` decodes a singleton machine with its optional keys left out as with them written at their defaults, and pins that machine's hazards and failure probabilities bit for bit. The committed machines still round-trip through the encoder (`crates/togi-sim/tests/suite/encode.rs`).
- **Fixed seed and workers:** the same seeded draws fit to bit-identical machines on one worker and on four (`search.rs`), and the ensemble and anchor stay byte-identical on every pool size (`crates/togi-tool-fit/tests/fit/determinism.rs`).

## Metrics

Against the unchanged prefix-fitted reference and the prefix constant, on the same host: pooled loss, F1 surprises, F2 `|ln(predicted / failures)|` (predicted count where a subset has no failures), F3 majority of development held-out sessions improved, rows for single-core, non-R7 and R7 trials, and forward elapsed time. F4 and F5 run once, on the winner, as finalization.

## Keep and stop

- Each candidate has one falsifiable sentence and a development-only estimate that the trials it addresses carry more than 0.005 loss per trial; otherwise it is not run.
- Freeze one dev winner whose primary improves while F1–F3 hold. Its one confirmation keeps it at a loss ratio of at most 1.10 with no rise in surprises; report the paired-trial bootstrap 95% interval, but the 1.10 point bar decides. F4 adds no newly flagged group on the approved artifact; F5 pairs every committed-machine run `equal`.
- Stop when the queue is exhausted, a limit is reached, no remaining candidate can contribute 0.005 per trial, or the confirmation fails. A failed confirmation ends the campaign.
- No model is kept because #427 decided its direction, and no result claims #480's R7 bar.

## Prerequisites
