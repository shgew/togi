# Brief: #480, why the shared-voltage forward score failed

**Mode:** model development, diagnostic only, under [the model program](../program.md) and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

The frozen shared-voltage candidate failed its once-only score: on both spent sessions its R7 loss exceeded the prefix constant, and on the live cut it predicted 4.12 times the observed failures ([the frozen score](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#the-2026-10-17-frozen-score)). Which of three causes explains overprediction on the development folds: the optimizer stopping at a local minimum, parameters the evidence cannot identify, or a physical assumption the model makes that the data contradict? The campaign ends at a frozen candidate and a report. It is not #480's next score.

## Candidate queue

One diagnostic hypothesis at a time, at most 12, each written into the manifest before it runs and naming the cause it tests:

- **Optimizer:** the fit from several predeclared starting points reaches materially different objectives on the same development fold.
- **Identifiability:** synthetic evidence drawn from known shared-voltage parameters, with the development folds' profiles and counts, does not recover those parameters or their R7 predictions.
- **Physics:** one accepted limit of #480's model (no failure-time shape, unloaded cores' offsets not changing an R7 load's hazard, y-cruncher sharing the higher mprime threshold) explains the development overprediction when relaxed.

A fix is a candidate only after its diagnostic names the cause it answers.

## Data

- **Development:** the six sessions through 20261002T235548Z of the program's [approved artifact](../program.md#the-data), and synthetic fixtures with known parameters.
- **Forbidden:** 20261004T000701Z, which #480's first candidate already scored; the spent score sessions 20261004T211726Z and 20261010T230611Z; every later session; and anything their scores suggested. The campaign has no confirmation allowance.

## Writable paths

`crates/togi-sim/src/`, `crates/togi-sim/tests/`, `crates/togi-tool-fit/src/` except `forward.rs`, `docs/simulating.md`, `docs/benchmarking.md`, and the campaign's output directory.

## Metrics

Per development held-out session: R7 log loss per start against the prefix constant and predicted against observed R7 failures, #480's per-session bar. Pooled loss and F1–F3 as [the program](../program.md#the-metric) defines them. For identifiability, the recovered parameters and R7 predictions against their known values. Every candidate is compared with the unchanged frozen fitter on the same host.

## Keep and stop

- A diagnostic is answered when its prediction holds or fails on its predeclared measure; both are results.
- A fix is kept as the frozen candidate only if it meets #480's per-session bar on every development R7 session and F1–F3 hold.
- Stop when the queue is exhausted or a limit is reached. Never score a spent or later session.

## Prerequisites

The approved artifact, materialized and validated, with spent and later sessions excluded and default facts paths unable to reach them (#427's readiness unit 427-A), and the paired per-trial forward output with full-precision aggregates (427-B). Further diagnostic tooling (objective decomposition, optimizer traces) lands only for a diagnostic the owner approves, before the campaign that needs it.

## Result

The campaign ledger and report: each diagnostic's hypothesis, measure and outcome, the frozen candidate's revision and exact development scores if one exists, and what remains unexplained. The result is "development result, unconfirmed"; it does not complete #480. A frozen candidate is the revision the report names, not a kept model: it opens no model pull request, since this campaign has no confirmation to report. Scoring it needs new sessions recorded after its freeze, through a separate owner-scheduled step, and any regeneration follows only a pass.
