# Brief: #490, intermittent R7 hazard under the current policy

**Mode:** fixed-policy observation, under [the tuner program](../program.md)'s frozen environment and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

Under the unchanged Ruleset 12 policy, how often does an intermittent multi-core R7 hazard let a part pass by chance, and how often does it cost permanent backoffs before the conclusion? What would asking for more evidence cost? The answer is a sensitivity study across hand-set hazards, not a fitted estimate of the target machine and not a replacement rule.

## Candidate queue

One fixed grid, written into the manifest before the first run: hazards of 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 failures per hour, each applied once as a background hazard and once as a profile-dependent hazard on the loaded cores. The two forms coincide at 0, so the grid is 15 fixtures, with 24 deterministic development seeds each. Durations and pass counts are the current configuration's. The default 12-candidate limit counts one hazard level, both forms, as one candidate, so the eight levels fit; the grid is not extended or refined from its results.

## Data

- **Permitted:** the hand-set synthetic fixtures, the unchanged tuner and configuration, the frozen research suite and its development seeds, and the existing evidence formulas.
- **Forbidden:** a fitted target hazard, any likelihood from #480's spent score sessions, fresh target evidence, and the published real traces as calibration. Ruleset 10's seed-107 trace is context, not a baseline.

## Writable paths

The campaign's output directory only. The tuner, configuration, simulator, standard suite, machine files and baseline stay unchanged.

## Metrics

- The exact probability that a fixed profile satisfies a part's short and long requirements, from the simulator's duration-aware failure probabilities with background included, as products over the required trials. The 120 s and 300 s requirements are not identical draws, and one attempt's pass probability is not the chance of an eventual passing streak.
- Over the simulated runs: completion within the boot cap, pre-conclusion failures, charged backoffs, depth changes and time, each as a distribution, and the share censored.
- The projected extra launches and time for predeclared larger evidence counts, labelled counterfactual: no tuner runs with them.

## Keep and stop

There is no tuner gate and nothing to win. The result is kept when the calculator's checks pass (probability 0 and 1, mixed durations, censored horizons, analytically solvable cases) and every grid point is reported. Stop when the grid is done or a limit is reached; censored runs stay in the result.

## Prerequisites

#490's readiness units: the offline pass and cost calculator in `togi-tool-bench` (490-A), backoff attribution with the phase and conclusion boundary in `togi-tool-stats` and the research-only synthetic fixtures (490-B). The frozen suite, seed and grid hashes enter the manifest.

## Result

The campaign ledger and a report for #490: calculator inputs and assumptions, every grid point's numbers, the distributions, censoring, and how the results depend on the simulator's model, with bounded design options, no change included. It is not a Ruleset 13 change: tolerated failures, hazard-derived evidence requirements and pre-conclusion pairing need a validated #480 score and an owner-approved design under #979.
