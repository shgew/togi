# Brief: #490, intermittent R7 hazard under the current policy

**Mode:** fixed-policy observation, under [the tuner program](../program.md)'s frozen environment and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

Under the unchanged Ruleset 12 policy, how often does an intermittent multi-core R7 hazard let a part pass by chance, and how often does it cost permanent backoffs before the conclusion? What would asking for more evidence cost? The answer is a sensitivity study across hand-set hazards, not a fitted estimate of the target machine and not a replacement rule.

## Candidate queue

One candidate, `grid`: the frozen research suite `490/suite.json`, run once on its development split. The grid is fixed by its files and is not extended or refined from its results:

- Hazards of 0, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 failures per hour, each in two forms: `background-H`, a CO-independent hazard, and `depth-H`, a profile-dependent one. Both forms at 0 are the same machine, `zero`, so the suite has 15 scenarios.
- Every machine is the seeded default machine (16 cores, two CCDs, limits drawn from the seed) plus the simulator's `ccd` hazard. That hazard applies to each R7 trial on each CCD it loads: `background-H` at H per hour whatever the offsets, `depth-H` at H per hour at a mean CCD depth of 25 counts, doubling every 5 counts deeper and halving every 5 shallower (`slope` ln 2/5). The mean is over all the CCD's cores, loaded or not. An all-core trial sees two CCDs' hazard; a single-core R7 trial sees its CCD's. The machines have no joints and no onset boost.
- 24 deterministic development seeds, 1 to 24, for each scenario: 360 sessions. Durations and pass counts are the current configuration's.

One run of the whole suite is one substantive candidate, so the default 12-candidate limit leaves the queue room for nothing else; there is no second candidate.

## Forbidden runtime changes

The campaign measures the policy as it ships, so none of these may change, in the tree, the configuration or a command line:

- the tuner (`crates/togi-tuner`), its configuration defaults, durations and pass counts, the run loop and the journal;
- the simulator, the bench and their flags beyond the suite, the split and the output paths;
- the fixtures and seeds above, the standard suite `tools/bench/suite.json`, its machines and `tools/bench/baseline.jsonl`.

No runtime tuner rule is added or tried, even as a counterfactual run: no evidence requirement derived from a hazard, no tolerated failure, and no pairing of failures before the conclusion. Those are design options for the report to bound, computed offline; each would need a validated #480 score and an owner-approved design under #979.

## Procedure

1. `just research 490 check --manifest FILE`, then `just research 490 start --manifest FILE`.
2. `just research 490 exec --run $RUN --candidate grid --role candidate -- bench --suite tools/bench/research/490/suite.json --split dev --keep $RUN/runs --out $RUN/records.jsonl`. The bench keeps each session's journal under `$RUN/runs/bench-*/<scenario>/dev-<seed>/` and stops each at its first clean cycle, so every session measures the policy up to its conclusion; a session at the boot cap is censored.
3. Read the kept journals, writing only under `$RUN`: for each session, `just stats --journal <session>/events.jsonl --backoffs $RUN/backoffs/<scenario>-<seed>.jsonl` gives every multi-core R7 failure with its phase, requirement and the backoffs and offset counts charged to it ([The stats tool](../../../docs/reviewing.md#the-stats-tool-togi-tool-stats)). The distributions are computed from these lines and `records.jsonl`, never from rendered tables.
4. `just pass-cost --machine tools/bench/research/490/machines/<scenario>.json --seed <seed> --profile <final profile> --extra-short 1,2,4` gives each part's exact probabilities at each session's final profile, background included, and the counterfactual cost of 1, 2 and 4 more short passes, the only larger evidence counts the report prices ([Counterfactual pass and cost](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#counterfactual-pass-and-cost-of-the-r7-requirements)). The machine form, not `--hazard`, is used because the fixtures' hazard is per loaded CCD, so the all-core part sees twice its level.
5. `just research 490 outcome --run $RUN --candidate grid --outcome kept|invalid --reason TEXT --metrics FILE`, `just research 490 stop --run $RUN --reason queue-exhausted`, then `just research 490 report --run $RUN --notes FILE`.

## Metrics

- The exact probability that a fixed profile satisfies a part's short and long requirements, from the simulator's duration-aware failure probabilities with background included, as products over the required trials. The 120 s and 300 s requirements are not identical draws, and one attempt's pass probability is not the chance of an eventual passing streak.
- Over the simulated runs: completion within the boot cap, pre-conclusion failures, charged backoffs, offset counts, depth changes and time, each as a distribution by scenario, and the share censored.
- The projected extra launches and time for 1, 2 and 4 more short passes per part, labelled counterfactual: no tuner runs with them.

## Keep and stop

There is no tuner gate and nothing to win. The outcome is `kept` when the calculator's checks pass (probability 0 and 1, mixed durations, censored horizons, analytically solvable cases) and every grid point is reported, and `invalid` otherwise. Stop when the grid is done or a limit is reached; censored runs stay in the result.

## Prerequisites

#490's readiness units: the offline pass and cost calculator in `togi-tool-bench` (490-A, #1079), backoff attribution with the phase and conclusion boundary in `togi-tool-stats` and the research fixtures (490-B, #1106), and this brief's frozen inputs and acceptance (490-C).

## Result

The campaign ledger and a report for #490. The report is accepted only when it has:

- the commit, the manifest's hashes and the limits used;
- for each of the 15 scenarios, its 24 sessions' completion, censoring, and the distributions of pre-conclusion multi-core R7 failures, failures charged a backoff, backoffs, offset counts, final depth and time to conclusion, with every censored session counted and none dropped;
- the calculator's inputs, assumptions and checks, and each part's first-attempt probability at each hazard;
- the counterfactual cost of each predeclared larger evidence count, labelled as such;
- how the results depend on the simulator's model: the `ccd` hazard's form and pivot, the per-CCD doubling on all-core trials, the absence of joints and onset, and that the hazards are hand-set, not fitted;
- bounded design options with no change included.

It is not a Ruleset 13 change: tolerated failures, hazard-derived evidence requirements and pre-conclusion pairing need a validated #480 score and an owner-approved design under #979.
