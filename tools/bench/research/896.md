# Brief: #896, what re-derived R7 parts cost and cover

**Mode:** counterfactual accounting, under [the tuner program](../program.md)'s frozen environment and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

How much of the unchanged tuner's re-derivation cost after mid-cycle backoffs repeats intermediate-order work, and how much is coverage of the final profile that nothing else provides? The answer bounds what another rule could save. #896's historical night (153 re-derivations, 22 trials and 3,615 s on steps 1 and 2) is quoted, not reproduced, and its re-tests found a real failure (trial 0278) that the old order never required: the hour is not an established safe saving.

## Candidate queue

Three counterfactuals, written into the manifest before the first run and accounted offline only:

1. Coalesce repeated derivations before returning to a step.
2. Defer earlier steps' re-tests until the cycle's last R7 step.
3. Tie-aware ordering: re-test only sets whose request order changed by more than a tie.

Each is one candidate. A trace with events removed is not what the alternative tuner would have done; where skipped telemetry or a skipped failure would change later order or the profile, the case stays unresolved.

## Data

- **Permitted:** one frozen corpus of native journals from the unchanged tuner over a dedicated development suite, scripted order, tie and backoff fixtures, and their reference evidence.
- **Forbidden:** a fresh target run, Go-journal compatibility, model feedback from #480's spent sessions, and the committed facts extract as a chain journal: it holds no chain events.

## Writable paths

The campaign's output directory only. The tuner, configuration, simulator, standard suite, machine files and baseline stay unchanged.

## Metrics

Per chain event, attributed to cycle, step, CCD, part and profile change: first derivation, unchanged re-derivation, changed class, a class valid evidence already covers, newly executed work and sets later superseded. Separately: event overhead, passing exposure, failed exposure, rerun obligations and recovery, in trials and seconds. Per counterfactual: the upper bound of avoidable seconds per run and as a fraction of time to conclusion, the final chain's coverage witnesses (exact class, profile and evidence), observed failures it would lose or delay, and unidentifiable decisions. Rerun time that is also cycle time counts once.

## Keep and stop

Nothing is kept on time alone. The result is kept when the accounting reconciles with the corpus's totals and every saving carries its coverage witnesses, even if the safe saving is zero. Stop when the corpus and fixtures are accounted or a limit is reached.

## Prerequisites

#896's readiness units: chain and trial cost attribution in `togi-tool-stats` (896-A), the conservative counterfactual and coverage analyzer (896-B), and the frozen native corpus with its recipe (896-C). The corpus hash enters the manifest.

## Result

The campaign ledger and a report for #896: structured per-event attribution, the counterfactuals' bounds with witnesses and uncertainty, and bounded design options. Replacing ADR 0055's rule needs a validated #480 score, an owner-approved design under #979 and a new ADR.
