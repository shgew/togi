# Brief: #896, what re-derived R7 parts cost and cover

**Mode:** counterfactual accounting, under [the tuner program](../program.md)'s frozen environment and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

How much of the unchanged tuner's re-derivation cost after mid-cycle backoffs repeats intermediate-order work, and how much is coverage of the final profile that nothing else provides? The answer bounds what another rule could save. #896's historical night (153 re-derivations, 22 trials and 3,615 s on steps 1 and 2) is quoted, not reproduced, and its re-tests found a real failure (trial 0278) that the old order never required: the hour is not an established safe saving.

## Candidate queue

One candidate, `corpus`: the frozen research suite `896/suite.json`, run once on its development split, with every session it keeps accounted under three counterfactuals predeclared here and in [Counterfactual re-derivation accounting](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#counterfactual-re-derivation-accounting):

1. **coalesce:** coalesce repeated derivations before returning to a step.
2. **defer:** defer earlier steps' re-tests until the cycle's last R7 step.
3. **tie:** tie-aware ordering, re-testing only sets whose request order changed by more than a tie.

The three are accounted offline from the same journals, so they share the one run; none is a tuner change or a run of its own. A trace with events removed is not what the alternative tuner would have done; where skipped telemetry or a skipped failure would change later order or the profile, the case stays unresolved. The queue is not extended or refined from its results, though the default 12-candidate limit would allow it.

## Procedure

1. At the manifest's checkout, `just focus togi-tool-stats` passes: the fixtures hold. A failure is a missing prerequisite, and the campaign does not start.
2. `just research 896 check --manifest FILE`, then `just research 896 start --manifest FILE`.
3. `just research 896 exec --run $RUN --candidate corpus --role candidate -- bench --suite tools/bench/research/896/suite.json --split dev --keep $RUN/runs-N --out $RUN/records.jsonl`, with N the exec attempt counted from 1, so a retried exec's sessions stay apart from those a failed one kept. The bench keeps each session's journal under `$RUN/runs-N/bench-*/<scenario>/dev-<seed>/` and stops each at its first clean cycle. A session's status is `concluded`, `deadend`, `censored` at the boot cap, or `error`; every status is a result.
4. `just counterfactuals $RUN/runs-N $RUN/accounting`, with N the complete exec's attempt, writing only under `$RUN`: for each kept session `<scenario>-<seed>`, `just stats --chains` writes the report and each chain derivation's attribution (`.report.txt`, `.chains.jsonl`; [The stats tool](../../../docs/reviewing.md#the-stats-tool-togi-tool-stats)), and `just stats --counterfactual --witnesses` writes the three candidates' table and records (`.counterfactual.txt`, `.witnesses.jsonl`; [Counterfactual re-derivation accounting](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#counterfactual-re-derivation-accounting)). A session the stats tool cannot read, such as an `error` session that left no journal, gets no files: the recipe accounts the rest, then names it and exits 1. The figures are computed from the JSON Lines and `records.jsonl`, never from rendered tables.
5. `just research 896 outcome --run $RUN --candidate corpus --outcome kept|invalid --reason TEXT --metrics FILE`, `just research 896 stop --run $RUN --reason queue-exhausted`, then `just research 896 report --run $RUN --notes FILE`.

## Metrics

Per chain event, attributed to cycle, step, CCD, part and profile change: first derivation, unchanged re-derivation, changed class, a class valid evidence already covers, newly executed work and sets later superseded. Separately: event overhead, passing exposure, failed exposure, rerun obligations and recovery, in trials and seconds. Per counterfactual: the upper bound of avoidable seconds per run and as a fraction of time to conclusion, the final chain's coverage witnesses (exact class, profile and evidence), observed failures it would lose or delay, and unidentifiable decisions. Rerun time that is also cycle time counts once.

The outcome's metrics file holds, per counterfactual, the corpus totals of its summaries' seconds (`trial_s`, `kept_s`, `skipped_s`, `required_s`, `avoidable_s`, `unresolved_s`), its `unresolved` derivations, the decisions it leaves unidentified, its `lost` and `delayed` failures and its `cycles_covered` and `cycles_unresolved`, keyed `<candidate>_<field>`.

## Keep and stop

Nothing is kept on time alone. The outcome is `kept` when every session the recipe could not account has status `error`, and, for every accounted session and counterfactual, the summary's kept, skipped and unresolved seconds add up to the chain trial seconds of the session's `--chains` attribution, and every skipped second counted avoidable lies in a covered cycle with its coverage witnesses, even if the safe saving is zero; it is `invalid` otherwise. Stop when the corpus is accounted or a limit is reached; censored and errored sessions stay in the result.
