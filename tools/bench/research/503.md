# Brief: #503, can a tuner learn its own machine's risk

**Mode:** fixed-policy observation, feasibility only, under [the tuner program](../program.md)'s frozen environment and [Research campaigns](../../../docs/benchmarking.md#research-campaigns). Only the owner starts this campaign.

## Question

Can an online learner that reads only its own session's journal make useful held-out predictions of failure risk and final depth from sparse trials, on synthetic machines, and replay to the same predictions deterministically? The learner predicts; it selects no offsets. #503's model shape, risk target, exploration, display confidence and purity stay open, and no simulated result settles them.

## Candidate queue

At most 12 learners, written into the manifest before the first runs, each with one falsifiable sentence about what it predicts better than the fixed reference: a predictor that reads the same journal prefix and predicts the prefix's observed failure rate as every trial's risk and the offset the tuner holds at that prefix as the final depth.

## Data

- **Permitted:** journals of the unchanged shipped tuner on synthetic machines with known parameters, read as journal prefixes in order.
- **Forbidden:** constants fitted to the owner's machine, the committed facts extract and fitted target machines as learner inputs, feedback from #480's spent sessions, and fresh target evidence.

## Writable paths

The campaign's output directory and the research-only learner module the prerequisite below names.

## Metrics

Against the fixed shipped tuner's outcome on each held-out synthetic session: calibration of predicted failure risk against the simulator's known hazard, error of the predicted final depth, prediction error by prefix length, and identical predictions when the journal is replayed. Time, crashes and hazard of the shipped tuner's runs are context.

## Keep and stop

A learner is a positive result when its predictions beat the fixed reference on the predeclared measures and replay exactly; a negative is equally complete. Stop when the queue is exhausted or a limit is reached.

## Prerequisites

None of the evaluation exists yet: streaming synthetic prefixes, exact journal reconstruction checks and calibration and depth-error reporting land in one readiness unit the owner approves for this scope, which also names where the learners live. Until it lands, the campaign stops before its first candidate.

## Result

The campaign ledger and a report for #503. It changes no runtime policy: production offset selection, failure-point expiry, a risk target, confidence promises and stored model state need their own decisions on #503.
