### Changed

- The day service now applies the committed permitted Ruleset 14 profile, including nonzero offsets, through durable intent, write and all-core readback instead of forcing stock. Same-boot restart, sleep resume and pause expiry or early resume reapply the still-permitted profile without promotion; pause, stop and emergency recovery retain zeroing. Successor scores are research, not an activation gate; hardware acceptance is the first live step's on-machine check.
- A cold boot that starts paused applies stock and, after resume, reapplies the committed profile but cannot promote in that boot, including a guarded learning-step restoration.
