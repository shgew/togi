### Added

- `togi night` shows what the night will do, asks, then reboots into the tuning boot without the GRUB menu; the night runs until you end it, and crash reboots stay in it.
- `F10` in the tuning dashboard asks to reboot into the day cycle, and `n` in a root `togi watch` asks to reboot into the night.
- `togi night-end`, an internal command of the tuning boot.

### Changed

- Every return from the tuning boot, the power button, a dead end and the restart limit included, restores the boot selection `togi night` replaced instead of selecting the first menu entry.
