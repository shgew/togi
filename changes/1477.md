### Added

- The day service applies stock CO 0 through durable intent, write and all-core readback transactions on normal boot, same-boot restart and resume, and verifies zero before marking a clean stop; automatic nonzero day offsets remain disabled.

### Changed

- `togi day record` hands a record to the running day service instead of requiring it to stop, and writes the journal directly only when no service runs. The service refuses a record drafted from another session.

### Fixed

- Emergency zeroing refuses before any SMU command when slot mapping failed, including when the day service holds the driver directly.
