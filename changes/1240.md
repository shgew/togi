### Added

- `services.togi.day.watchdog` stops PID 1's hardware watchdog before every suspend or hibernation and restarts it on resume, so a sleep longer than the watchdog timeout cannot reset the machine.
- The day observer records in `day.boot_start` whether PID 1's hardware watchdog is armed, and `togi status` shows it.

### Changed

- A watchdog reset whose crashed boot ended at a suspend or hibernation entry is now classified as sleep-related instead of falling through to the tail or reset-reason classes, and the day question says the log ended at a sleep entry.
