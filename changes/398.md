### Changed

- `togi watch` uses the whole screen: a stage line, the running trial with what each outcome leads to as the tuner forecasts it, both CCDs side by side, and the cycle checklist, hunt parts and probes, or search turns beside what happened.
- `togi watch` redraws when the journal changes instead of rereading it every second, and holds the screen still through each R6 idle trial until its end is recorded.

### Fixed

- A crash inside the dashboard of `togi run` no longer stops tuning: the dashboard is hidden and event lines are printed instead.
