### Added

- `togi status --evidence` shows exact-profile and stock observations, rate/slope witnesses with their loaded cores, qualification hours, provenance and coverage gaps without a verdict or progress promise; a night-qualified profile is inspectable without the day service. Status and watch also explain the Ruleset 14 day state: the applied profile D, the confirmed ceiling C and the unit edge U, the hold and any blocker or stop, with the statistical S shown only as a diagnostic apart from them.
- `togi day notify` and status show a journal-derived morning report on the first observed day boot after a night, explicitly naming the stock-only application restriction without claiming current offsets were verified.
- `togi doctor --day` checks day prerequisites and this boot's complete cited CO-0 baseline/restoration receipts separately from applied D or current readback.
