### Added

- `togi day`, enabled with `services.togi.day.enable`, records each normal boot's offsets, its applied hours, clean stops, and unclean ends with their reset reason, without writing the SMU.
- `togi status` shows a Day section with boots observed, applied hours per offset profile, and clean and unclean ends.
- `services.togi.day.watchdog` arms a 60 s hardware watchdog in normal boots with the day service; it is off by default.
