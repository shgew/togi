### Added

- `just fit-artifact FILE` writes the original seven-session research artifact after checking its hash.

### Changed

- `just fit` needs `--facts`, and `--out` when it writes machine files, and reads sessions recorded after the original research artifact only with `--current-extract`.
- Without `--current-extract`, an extract holding any of the original research artifact's sessions must be that exact artifact, and `just fit --forward-only --reference-machine` needs `--current-extract`.
- Every fit and forward-check report opens with the `Facts: sha256` of the rows it read, and a run on the original research artifact says whether it scored or sealed the historical confirmation session.
