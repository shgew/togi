### Changed

- `togi watch` has nine pages that Left and Right cycle through (Main, Cores, Cycle, R7 evidence, Hunts / probes, BIOS profile, History, Journal and Help); `Esc` or `m` returns to Main, and the footer names the page and its place.
- The dashboard's Main page shows the phase and its progress in steps, cores or credited checks, the running operation with a 24-cell trial bar, every core's tuning-profile offset as a one-count gauge with its recorded failure in a separate lane, and a short What happened with routine passes folded.
- The full dashboard now needs 160×45; smaller terminals show the same per-core values as a numeric table, and terminals under 80×30 scroll it.
- The dashboard's Journal page folds a trial's passed self-tests into one row and shows profile changes as the cores that moved.
