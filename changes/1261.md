### Changed

- **BREAKING** The first `togi run` after updating archives the current session and starts a new one that maps from scratch, keeping the raw day observations, because Ruleset 13 now tests at night and steps the day profile one CCD at a time; its first night runs stock controls before mapping, see [how togi tunes](../docs/how-togi-tunes.md).
- **BREAKING** A night has no end time: `togi run` tunes until you end the night with Ctrl-C, `F10`, the power button or a reboot, or until a dead end stops it, so remove `--until`, `--cycles` and `--first-result` from anything that runs it.
- **BREAKING** An unresolved too-aggressive known-defect finding stops the run as a dead end (exit 17) until `togi reset --all`, instead of asking to reset the affected cores.

### Removed

- **BREAKING** The settings `start_offsets`, `candidate_solo_limits`, `durations.search_trial_s`, `durations.short_trial_s`, `durations.checking_trial_s`, `durations.checking_idle_s`, `durations.checking_all_core_s`, `evidence.miss`, `evidence.rate` and `checking.cycle` are retired: a configuration that sets one fails to load with an error naming it, so remove them.
- **BREAKING** `togi reset --core` and `togi reset --config` are removed; `togi reset --all` archives the session and starts over.
