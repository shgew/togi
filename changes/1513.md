### Fixed

- Night mapping keeps each step's declared partial-load subsets, continues its original task, reuses compatible completed cells and preserves CCD alternation instead of rewinding after later voltage telemetry.
- Completed stock controls stay complete within their firmware and evidence context when later telemetry changes partial-load subsets.
- Recognized historical mapping rewinds receive a too-cautious defect finding and can continue under the fixed policy without discarding their recorded evidence.
