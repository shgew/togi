### Changed

- The `togi day record` confirmation says the tuner treats the record as whole-profile evidence and a veto when choosing day steps and qualifications, and that this build applies no day profile at runtime.

### Fixed

- A `togi day record` of a crash no day observer saw reaches a durable `day.event` and `day.veto`, so it steps the day profile back, gets its reproduction night and stops blocking promotion once those are done. A session that already holds such a record keeps the wrong retreat and escalation history from before this fix: run `togi reset --all` to archive it and start over.
