- **BREAKING** Ruleset 14 two-ends tuning replaces statistical-cap permission: every bounded day step needs an exact-profile 50-hour hold and night qualification beneath C; status/watch show D/C/U, holds, stops and experimental fixed-rate evidence rather than remaining permission to S. - Day pause preserves Ruleset 14 learning and saved D while status/watch distinguish verified stock from the saved profile. Paused intervals never earn the 50-hour hold; a cold boot starting paused cannot promote or restore after resume, and a crash observed at paused CO 0 neither consumes the campaign allowance nor stops a tuning unit.

- Status and watch explain the applied profile D, confirmed ceiling C, unit edge U, hold and blockers or stops; the statistical S remains a separate diagnostic, never permission.
- A crash from before the machine's first campaign enrollment, whether recorded live, imported from a Ruleset 13 session or appended by hand after the transition, vetoes its profile and deeper ones but never consumes the campaign allowance, stops a unit or needs applied-step history; a late record still forces a safe retreat from a forbidden applied profile without stopping units solely for that veto. Crashes at or after enrollment keep the existing charge, stop and ancestry rules.
