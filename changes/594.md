### Fixed

- `togi watch` no longer says a trial has ended before the first trial, no longer offers another confirmation trial once a core's confirmation is complete, and no longer shows a running stage or `repeats until stopped` after the session stopped.
- `togi watch` says an unfinished trial crashed, and that `togi run` records it, when a later boot has written events, for every regime including R6; an earlier trial's recovery or a reset command's later clean shutdown no longer masks that crash.
- An overdue non-R6 trial shows how long it is past its planned end without claiming a crash, and says no trial end is recorded even when same-boot progress follows it; started R6 trials retain their frozen planned-end frame in live, one-frame and replay views, even past that end.
- `togi watch` shows `can't read journal: <reason>` for a journal it cannot read, with a retry promise in the live dashboard only; `no session yet` is left for a journal that does not exist.
