# Contributing

togi is developed in the open, but it does not take contributions of any kind. One person designs, builds and maintains it.

- **Issues, comments, reactions and pull requests** are limited to the repository's collaborators. Nobody else can post here, and nothing sent elsewhere about togi is taken up.
- **Offers to pay for work** on togi are declined.
- **Security:** there is no private reporting channel.

To take togi somewhere else, fork it; the [GPL](LICENSE) allows it, as long as a fork you distribute keeps the same license and publishes its source.
