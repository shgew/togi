# Contributing

togi is open source, but not open to contributions yet. Until 1.0 its design still moves quickly, and one person keeps it coherent.

- **Bug reports are welcome.** Open an issue with the Bug template: what happened and what you expected. If available, include the togi version (`togi --version`), kernel (`uname -r`), CPU model, board with BIOS and AGESA version, `ryzen_smu` version, and an excerpt from `togi events` around the problem, with the full journal on request. These details are optional; share what you know.
- **Pull requests are closed** to anyone but the maintainer, and feature requests are not taken. The Maintainer templates are for the plan the maintainer keeps in issues.
- **Security issues:** report them privately through the repository's Security tab, not in a public issue.

This will be revisited at 1.0. Until then, forking is the way to take togi somewhere else; the [GPL](LICENSE) allows it, as long as a fork you distribute keeps the same license and publishes its source.
