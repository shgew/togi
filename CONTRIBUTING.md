# Contributing

togi is open source, but not open to contributions yet. Until 1.0 its design still moves quickly, and one person keeps it coherent.

- **Bug reports are welcome.** Open an issue with the Bug template: what happened, what you expected, the togi version and the journal around it (`togi events`).
- **Pull requests are closed** to anyone but the maintainer, and feature requests are not taken. The Maintainer templates are for the plan the maintainer keeps in issues.
- **Security issues:** report them privately through the repository's Security tab, not in a public issue.

This will be revisited at 1.0. Until then, forking is the way to take togi somewhere else; the [MIT license](LICENSE) allows it.
